Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

I work on diffusion-based controllable image and video generation at UIUC, advised by Yu-Xiong Wang. I design the supervision, training data, and curricula that teach pretrained generative models new capabilities. So far these include new visual controls, multi-view and temporal consistency, and identity-consistent video at minute scale.

I am now training visual world models through multimodal post-training. They predict the next screen after a UI action, physical events from observed video, and robot–object interactions under a prescribed motion.

NeurIPS 2025First author

Virtual Fitting Room: Generating Arbitrarily Long Videos of Virtual Try-On from a Single Image

Jun-Kun Chen, Aayush Bansal, Minh Phuoc Vo, Yu-Xiong Wang

Learns reference-conditioned generation from ordinary pairs of short clips. At inference, generated 360° anchors keep identity consistent in try-on videos up to 90 seconds long. Evaluated against D&D+FramePack and D&D+Kling 2.0 pipelines.

ConsistDreamer: 3D-Consistent 2D Diffusion for High-Fidelity Scene Editing

Jun-Kun Chen, Samuel Rota Bulò, Norman Müller, Lorenzo Porzi, Peter Kontschieder, Yu-Xiong Wang

Teaches 2D diffusion to edit 3D scenes consistently across views. Uses cross-view self-supervision and staged adaptation, with consistency losses along the whole denoising trajectory. An asynchronous multi-GPU pipeline overlaps diffusion training and image generation with scene fitting.

Current Research: Generative World Models

Research direction: connect language reasoning with visual generation, and use the outcomes the model predicts as training feedback.

Task example from the training data: screen + tap → next screen.

Computer-use visual world model

Image diffusion · SenseNova MoT

Built a next-screen world model on SenseNova MoT: a language Reasoner describes what an action changes and what must stay the same, and a diffusion Generator adapted through diffusion post-training renders the next screenshot. Ongoing work trains the Reasoner on a teacher's corrections of the model's own rendering mistakes.

Task example from PhysicsIQ: past video → future video.

Physical-event world model

Video diffusion · Cosmos3

Adapted Cosmos3 video diffusion to predict physical events from past RGB frames, conditioned on structured event descriptions. Implemented teacher distillation: a teacher that sees the whole video supervises a language Reasoner that sees only the past frames. An earlier online variant also updated the Generator from generated-versus-real comparisons.

Held-out DROID scene: predicted (left) vs. real (right) interaction video.

Robot-interaction world model

Motion-conditioned video diffusion · Cosmos3

Trained a motion-conditioned video world model on simulated and real robot data (MimicGen, RoboTwin, DROID, RH20T, AgiBot). It predicts robot–object interaction video from a starting scene, recent frames, and a rendering of the prescribed robot motion. Ongoing work studies adaptation to new robot and camera setups without retraining.
