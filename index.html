About

I work on controllable generative models and visual world models. My research spans image and video diffusion, multimodal conditioning and post-training, long-horizon generation, and large-scale generative-model training.

I am advised by Prof. Yu-Xiong Wang. Previously, I completed two research internships at Meta Reality Labs and worked at SpreeAI. I have first- or co-first-author papers at NeurIPS, CVPR, and 3DV.

Current Research

I am studying how language-model reasoning and visual generative priors can interact in both directions—so visual predictions can inform subsequent reasoning, rather than serving only as one-way decoded outputs.

Computer-use world model

Image diffusion · mixture of transformers

Developing an instruction-conditioned SenseNova image-diffusion model that predicts outcome screenshots for visual look-ahead in computer-use agents.

I lead diffusion post-training and the joint reasoner–generator design, including supervision that feeds predicted visual outcomes back into the reasoning loop.

Physical-event world model

Video diffusion · multimodal reasoning

Developing a Cosmos-based video world model that predicts future physical events from observed RGB video.

The post-training design uses a multimodal teacher to compare generated and target outcomes and to improve both reasoning and visual generation.

Robot-interaction world model

Motion-conditioned video diffusion

Building a video world model that predicts robot–object interactions from an initial observation and prescribed robot motion.

The current synthetic prototype studies proxy-to-target correspondence across robot embodiments and evaluates trajectories, contacts, and task outcomes.
